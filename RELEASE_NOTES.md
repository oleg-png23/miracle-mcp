Miracle 2.1.0 refreshes the extension and introduces its companion website.

- Lighter liquid glass: two focal optical surfaces, smaller cached maps and a precomputed refraction profile. Offscreen lenses suspend their filters.
- New Coast, Alps and Aurora wallpapers shared by the extension and website. Optimized WebP files replace the default heavy landscape downloads.
- All eight active wallpapers total about 1.27 MB. Release archives omit the unused 4K source duplicates.
- Redesigned welcome page with live bridge status, direct setup guidance and a clearer path into the workspace.
- Welcome opens on browser startup or extension launch at most once every five hours. Launches during cooldown open the workspace; browser startup stays quiet. Existing pages are reused.
- The Miracle logo stays anchored inside the navigation rail during scrolling.
- A responsive, self-contained website with installation instructions, downloads and an interactive appearance preview.

Downloads:

- `miracle-mcp-2.1.0.zip`: extract, load the `miracle-mcp` folder as an unpacked extension in Chrome/Edge, and run `run.bat` for the local Studio bridge.
- `miracle-website-2.1.0.zip`: the standalone static website, ready to serve.
- `miracle-wallpapers-2.1.0.zip`: the three new landscapes and their source notes.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: SHA-256 checksums for all archives.

The full refraction effect uses Chromium. Other browsers receive a frosted fallback. Reduced motion, system transparency defaults and forced colors are respected.
