### Generation prompts

Shared direction: `photorealistic-natural`, wide 16:9 browser wallpaper and website backdrop, authentic editorial landscape photography, restrained colors and natural textures, large calm upper and central regions so translucent glass panels remain readable, fine detail concentrated along the lower third and edges. No people, birds, buildings, boats, typography, labels, logos, watermark, or interface elements.

- **Coast:** Remote volcanic coastline inspired by the Faroe Islands. Sculptural black basalt cliffs curve from the lower right into the distance. Silky deep petrol teal Atlantic water, muted jade grassy cliff tops, layered coastal headlands fading into sea mist. Quiet cloudy early morning light, pale sea fog and smooth open ocean. Natural, physically believable scale and textures.
- **Alpine:** Glacial lake inspired by the Swiss Alps at misty dawn. Still luminous celadon teal water with gentle reflections, dark pine forest along the bottom and lower left, elegant alpine peaks toward the right and distance softened by silver atmospheric haze. Cool powder blue morning sky and wisps of fog between mountains. Authentic rock and evergreen detail, soft natural light.
- **Aurora:** A realistic long-exposure night landscape inspired by a remote Norwegian fjord. One graceful ribbon of naturally emerald aurora sweeps diagonally across a deep midnight teal starry sky. Rugged low mountain silhouettes and still dark water in the bottom third catch a faint green reflection. Restrained brightness, rich charcoal navy shadows, subtle stars, no purple neon overload or moon.
