# Playground concept images

These fictional environment images were generated for the Miracle website on 2026-10-20 using the built-in image_gen tool. They are generated concept art, not screenshots of a connected Studio session. No vector scene illustration is used in the gallery.

Final website assets:

- [sky.webp](sky.webp) — 1280-pixel image; [sky-640.webp](sky-640.webp) — mobile-sized version.
- [neon.webp](neon.webp) — 1280-pixel image; [neon-640.webp](neon-640.webp) — mobile-sized version.
- [moon.webp](moon.webp) — 1280-pixel image; [moon-640.webp](moon-640.webp) — mobile-sized version.

The exported WebP files preserve the generated compositions; resizing and encoding prepare the images for the website. Original generated PNGs are retained in the conversation workspace.

## Generation prompts

### sky

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a wide landscape image for a premium Roblox Studio companion website, displayed as an immersive image in an interactive concept gallery. Create a cinematic, convincingly rendered environment with realistic PBR materials, atmospheric depth, rich detail and restrained filmic color. Camera at eye level or a natural elevated landscape viewpoint, not an isometric diagram, not a miniature diorama, not a flat vector illustration. Wide 16:9 composition. The scene must fill the entire image, no borders, no graphic layout, no UI, no words, no logos, no watermarks. Keep the key subject near the center with enough surrounding environment for responsive cropping. This is one of a coherent series of three environments: refined blue-gray atmosphere with pastel natural highlights, polished modern game-world art direction. Scene: a beautiful chain of large floating grassy islands above a vast sea of soft clouds, connected by delicate stone footbridges. A luminous pale stone arch portal stands on the nearest island, with small lush trees, weathered stone and a few warm light accents. Soft early morning light and a pale blue sky. A breathtaking inhabitable fantasy landscape, tactile grass and rock, strong photographic sense of scale. No characters.
