# Miracle landscapes

The three landscapes below are original AI-generated images created with the built-in image generation tool for Miracle. They are packaged locally as optimized WebP files at their native 1672 × 941 resolution, without upscaling.

- `coast-new.webp`: muted teal ocean, basalt coastal cliffs, soft daylight.
- `alpine-new.webp`: misty alpine lake and pale mountains.
- `aurora-new.webp`: emerald aurora over a quiet northern fjord.

These images are not photographs of specific locations. No external image requests are made by this website.
